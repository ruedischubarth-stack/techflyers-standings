How points are earned

  • Team meeting · 2 pts
  • Simulator practice · 2 pts — Once per week: weekly practice log submitted with the minimum practice met.
  • Live-flight practice · 3 pts
  • Social / campus event · 3 pts — Homecoming, STOMP, Hootenanny and other social events.
  • Official simulator race · 4 pts
  • Official IRL race · 4 pts
  • Controlled-flight milestone · 3 pts — Once per week: pilot answers Yes to "Did you meet it?" on the weekly log. Partly does not count.
  • Consistency / lap-time milestone · 5 pts — Once per week: best 3 consecutive laps at end of week faster than that week's first-session baseline by at least 5% (Starting goal) or 2% (Experienced pilot challenge, as the pilot selects on the form). Note both times.
  • Hosted race workday · 5 pts
  • Sponsor outreach assignment · 1 pts
